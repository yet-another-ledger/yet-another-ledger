# M20 · Fixed Asset Register, AI Residual Value Estimates, and AI API Protocol Selection

> 🌐 **English** · [中文](M20_zh.md)

> The author approved this design on 2026-11-23. The [roadmap](../roadmap.md#progress-tracker) tracks implementation and acceptance.

## Goal and scope

Keep a yearly fixed asset register for equipment such as the author's Fluke tester and home storage battery. Register an asset from a saved purchase or while confirming a new purchase, calculate straight-line depreciation with a residual value, and feed depreciation into P&L and the Dashboard. Offer a reviewable AI residual value estimate and a shared choice of Responses or Chat Completions for all application AI calls.

M20 covers one whole confirmed purchase Expense per asset. Combining purchases, splitting a purchase, importing prior-year opening balances, disposal, a general ledger or balance sheet, and live market searches for residual values are deferred.

## Frozen product and architecture decisions

### D1 · Purchase and registration

- In **New Expense** and **Receipt Inbox** review, offer an unchecked **Register as fixed asset** option. It opens the asset name, actual in-service date, useful life, estimated residual value and use type. Default to five years, zero residual value in company base currency and fully business use. For mixed use, collect an initial estimated business-use percentage before depreciation begins. An empty in-service date creates a pending-in-service asset. Entering the actual date starts the depreciation schedule.
- From a saved purchase, offer **Convert to fixed asset** with the same fields. Preview the old Expense contribution, new depreciation and differences by reporting period before confirmation. The existing Fluke purchase can be recalculated from its in-service date within its purchase year. If the old contribution or the replacement would affect P&L in an earlier calendar year, block the conversion. Existing purchases are converted individually after confirmation.
- Keep the original Expense, attachment ownership and purchase VAT. The asset takes over P&L recognition, including when a new asset is pending in service. Retain the original Expense's VAT and business-use inputs for existing VAT rules. Stage the purchase and linked asset in the same transaction. Inbox confirmation also transfers the originals in that transaction. Use the existing inbox lock and file revision guard. For ordinary new purchases with an asset, scope a client submission UUID to the company and return the existing result on an identical retry. Return a conflict if the UUID is reused with different data.
- Preserve the acquisition cost inputs and association. Protect the linked purchase's financial fields and deletion through ordinary Expense commands. Preview and audit controlled changes that affect depreciation or the underlying purchase. Keep original attachment and revision history available.

### D2 · Cost, depreciation and annual register

- Derive acquisition cost from the saved base-currency purchase amounts, with actual irrecoverable purchase VAT where the existing purchase treatment supports it. Reuse the purchase cost-basis logic and locked FX snapshot. The €450 reference is an informational cue. The user decides whether to capitalize a purchase. [Dutch Tax Administration guidance on acquisition cost and low-value assets](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/investeren_in_bedrijfsmiddelen).
- Use straight-line depreciation. Annual baseline equals `(acquisition cost − confirmed residual value) / useful life in years`. Accept a non-negative residual up to acquisition cost and a positive whole-year useful life. Validate the 20% annual baseline limit for the ordinary equipment covered by M20. Prorate the first month by actual days in use, allocate at month end, and settle the remaining fraction and minor-unit rounding in the final period. Full cumulative depreciation ends at acquisition cost less residual value. The register can show planned future depreciation. P&L and the Dashboard recognize the amounts for completed months in the reporting period. [Dutch Tax Administration guidance on calculating depreciation](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/afschrijving/hoe_berekent_u_het_bedrag_van_de_afschrijving).
- Show the source purchase, cost, estimated residual, opening book value, annual and cumulative depreciation, closing book value and use status by calendar year. Return independently identifiable fixed-asset depreciation in P&L and use the same projection in the Dashboard. Continue the existing legacy expense amortization for unconverted purchases.

### D3 · Mixed use and storage-battery VAT

- Fully business assets contribute 100% of depreciation to P&L. For mixed use, the current year's user-entered estimate contributes provisionally. A later year can carry forward the latest recorded percentage as an estimate until the user enters or confirms that year's percentage. Mark provisional contributions in the register, P&L and Dashboard. In Q4 or later, confirming the actual value replaces that calendar year's estimate and recalculates the affected months and quarters. Save before-and-after values, reason, actor and time on corrections. The register shows full depreciation, the private-use reduction and the net P&L effect. Book value uses full depreciation.
- The same purchase can have one fixed asset and one [M16 battery record](M16.md). Support either registration order, using the same in-service date when both records exist. An existing battery record supplies that date during conversion. The P&L use inputs and the VAT 1d annual private-use inputs are separately confirmed. The original purchase remains the sole source of purchase VAT, including the existing 5b contribution. The VAT battery adjustment continues using M16's basis and schedule.
- When a controlled revision affects their shared purchase basis or in-service date, preview the VAT and P&L consequences and update both records in one transaction. Annual revisions confined to one register affect its own projection. Retain M16's reconfirmation and provenance rules.

### D4 · AI protocol selection and compatibility

- Add the typed global setting `api_protocol` with `responses` and `chat_completions`. Precedence is an explicit stored value, then an explicit `AI_API_PROTOCOL` environment value. With neither, an existing AI settings row or existing environment credentials keeps Chat Completions. A fresh configuration defaults to Responses. An older API client omitting the field retains the effective protocol. Keep the existing Base URL, model, enabled flag, prompts and key storage/overlays. Read responses and logs never expose a key.
- Extend `GET/PUT /api/v1/settings/ai` and `POST /api/v1/settings/ai/test`. Show the effective protocol in Settings. Test unsaved overrides with a synthetic image using the same resolver and transport as the actual workflow. Saving is a separate action. When a model or endpoint rejects the request, report the selected protocol and safe error detail so the administrator can fix the configuration or switch explicitly.
- Append `/responses` or `/chat/completions` to the configured API root. Apply the transport selection to direct Expense attachments, receipt inbox, external sales, invoice PDF validation and residual estimates. Keep the existing PDF rasterization and common prompt and validation logic. Responses uses stateless non-streaming requests with `store: false`, image input and structured text configuration as supported. Read assistant output-text blocks from the typed output array and handle refusal, incomplete and empty output explicitly. If a provider explicitly rejects the requested JSON format, a single retry on the same protocol may omit that parameter and still enforce the application parser. Protocol errors never trigger a request on another protocol. [OpenAI migration guide](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/migrate-to-responses) · [OpenRouter Responses API](https://openrouter.ai/docs/api_reference/responses/overview).

### D5 · AI residual value estimates

- Enrich the existing purchase recognition response with optional product name, brand and model when readable. The estimate is an explicit button beside the residual field. Use recognized product details or manual input, the server-validated acquisition cost and useful life, plus any user-supplied condition or usage assumptions. Only those inputs are sent for the estimate. The original purchase file stays in the original receipt workflow.
- Return a suggested end-of-life amount in company base currency, a brief reason and material assumptions. Require a finite amount from zero through acquisition cost and validate the response on the backend. Return an insufficient-data state when the model lacks a credible product basis. Label the suggestion as a model estimate and show uncertainty. The user can apply, ignore or edit it. Changes to the item, cost or years invalidate the suggestion. A failed call leaves the user's amount intact. Persist the final confirmed form value.

## Contract and data model

| Surface | Contract |
| --- | --- |
| `/api/v1/fixed-assets` | List and read assets, preview and confirm conversion, preview and submit controlled corrections, and read or confirm annual use. The service supplies the authoritative schedule and report deltas. |
| New Expense and receipt confirmation | Accept the same optional fixed-asset input and return the associated asset ID on success. Older requests continue through the existing purchase path. |
| P&L and Dashboard | Add fixed asset depreciation and provisional-use information to the existing response. The expense report continues showing underlying purchase records. |
| AI settings and test | Add protocol selection through the existing typed settings APIs and cached settings service. The settings JSONB accepts missing keys, so protocol selection needs no database migration or key backfill. |
| Purchase extraction and residual estimate | Extend `ExpenseAIPrefill` additively with optional product data. Provide an authenticated estimate endpoint under the fixed-asset API. Existing extraction payloads remain readable. |

Use additive Alembic migrations for company-owned fixed assets, their annual use records and audit history. Use PostgreSQL RLS, same-company foreign keys, a unique source-purchase association and a unique durable submission identifier where applicable. Keep amounts as `Decimal` and monetary calculations in backend services. Regenerate OpenAPI TypeScript types after contract changes.

### Step 1 · Depreciation calculation and conversion preview

- **Goal / inputs** Deliver a read-only preview for existing purchases using D1–D2, M8.5 fields and M10 reporting.
- **Backend / contract** Lock the asset inputs, monthly and yearly schedule and conversion delta contract. Implement the Decimal calculations.
- **Frontend / migration** Show old amortization, new depreciation and the difference from Expense details. No migration.
- **Required tests** Zero and nonzero residuals, partial first year, leap dates, rounding, invalid life and residual above cost.
- **Blind-review points** Monthly and annual totals reconcile with closing book value. Preview performs no writes.

### Step 2 · Existing purchase conversion and annual register

- **Goal / inputs** Use Step 1 and D1–D2 to confirm fully business assets and show their annual register.
- **Backend / migration** Add RLS-protected asset records and the purchase association. Lock the Expense and revalidate preview when confirming. Protect linked financial fields and route depreciation through the shared P&L calculation.
- **Frontend** Add register navigation, annual list and details with values and source-purchase links.
- **Required tests** Same-year Fluke conversion, prior-year protection, month-end attribution, retry, concurrency, rollback, company isolation, purchase VAT and original receipts.
- **Blind-review points** The original P&L contribution is replaced exactly once. P&L and Dashboard reconcile with the register.

### Step 3 · Mixed use and battery linkage

- **Goal / inputs** Deliver D3 on top of Step 2 and the M16 battery register.
- **Backend / migration** Add annual estimates, confirmations and audit revisions. Support both registration orders and atomic shared-basis corrections.
- **Frontend** Show provisional values, annual confirmation deltas, revision history and the battery VAT entry point.
- **Required tests** Q4 confirmation recalculates Q1–Q3, yearly rates are independent, both registration orders work, and shared corrections reconcile. Exercise separate P&L and VAT use revisions.
- **Blind-review points** Each event affects the appropriate calendar periods and reports exactly once.

### Step 4 · Register a fixed asset while adding an Expense

- **Goal / inputs** Reuse Steps 2–3 from manual purchase creation and receipt inbox confirmation.
- **Backend / contract** Accept common optional asset input. Use ordinary creation replay and the existing inbox version guard. Commit the purchase, asset and applicable attachment transfer together.
- **Frontend** Add the asset option, defaults, pending-in-service display and a link to the saved asset.
- **Required tests** Unchecked path, both checked paths, default and custom residual, pending-in-service date, repeat submission, stale file version and rollback.
- **Blind-review points** One confirmation produces one purchase and one asset, and failure preserves a recoverable review form.

### Step 5 · Responses and Chat Completions selection

- **Goal / inputs** Implement D4 across the current AI callers, with backward-compatible upgrade behavior.
- **Backend / contract** Add protocol settings, shared transport selection and a synthetic image connectivity test. Handle image payloads, text extraction and provider failures for both protocols.
- **Frontend / migration** Add the protocol control, translated labels and a test of unsaved values. Keep existing settings and credentials readable without backfilling them.
- **Required tests** Fresh, legacy and environment-only configurations, explicit override, omitted field, switch and switch-back, image and JSON requests, refusal, truncation, malformed output and unsupported endpoint. Exercise a synthetic receipt through OpenRouter Responses in an isolated test configuration.
- **Blind-review points** Settings, connectivity tests and real calls resolve the same protocol. No cross-protocol automatic retry or credential/receipt disclosure.

### Step 6 · AI residual value estimates

- **Goal / inputs** Apply D5 using the Step 5 transport and M19 purchase recognition.
- **Backend / contract** Add optional product extraction and the estimate call with validated output and an insufficient-data state.
- **Frontend** Show the estimate, reason and assumptions, allow explicit application, and retain the manual path and user edits.
- **Required tests** Receipt and manual input, apply, ignore, edit, insufficient data, invalid amount, timeout, stale response and both protocols. Regress existing recognition and VAT suggestions.
- **Blind-review points** Estimation makes no booking and the stored residual is the confirmed form value.

- **Blind-review points** Reconcile each registration route, historical conversion, report projection, protocol upgrade and fallback to manual input. Distinguish automation from author acceptance.

## Deployment self-tests and acceptance

Use the default dev Compose command `docker compose -f docker-compose.yml -f docker-compose.dev.yml up -d` with isolated fixtures and AI configuration.

2. Add a purchase directly with default zero residual. Try an asset with a manually entered residual and leave its in-service date blank. Verify the register shows it awaiting use, then enter its actual date and verify month-end depreciation.

Verify the original, 5b, the M16 annual adjustment, provisional P&L, and the revised earlier quarters after Q4 confirmation.
4. Upgrade a fixture configured for Chat Completions. Test Responses before saving, switch using a supported model, recognize a synthetic receipt and switch back. Check a fresh configuration defaults to Responses, and verify manual entry when the provider is unavailable.
