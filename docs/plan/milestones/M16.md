## Goals & Scope

**Goal**: record sales documents supplied by platforms or energy companies, independently control their VAT and company P/L effects, handle five years of home-battery private-use adjustments, and expose the SI3 mileage/profit breakdown.

**IN**:
- External sales originals, manual entry, advisory AI prefill, explicit posting and searchable history.
- Dutch domestic ordinary sales, with one or more tax-treatment/rate summary rows per document.
- External credit documents, signed energy-settlement adjustments, audited bookkeeping revisions and voiding mistaken entries.
- Expense P/L inclusion, irrecoverable purchase VAT in the cost basis, and explicit legacy compatibility.
- Battery acquisition registration, in-service date, annual private-use inputs and Q4 box 1d.
- Consistent P/L, VAT and Dashboard projections; mileage amount and profit before/after mileage.

**OUT / deferred**:
- KOR, multiple VAT-return registrations, external EU/export/reverse-charge sales and their ICP reporting.
- Platform APIs, bank feeds, automatic settlement, batch payout allocation and accounts-receivable management.
- Automatic commission deduction, EMS integrations or authoritative income-tax classification.
- VAT filing, filed-period locks, automatic supplementary returns and general capital-goods adjustment rules.
- Foreign-currency/FX expansion; retain the existing EUR single-currency boundary.
- Issuing or sending another JAI Invoice for an imported original.

**Related foundations**: [roadmap](../roadmap.md), [VAT guide](../../insight/btw-aangifte-2026-guide.md), [M8](M8.md)/[M8.5](M8.5.md) Expenses, [M10](M10.md) reporting, [M11](M11.md) mileage, [M13](M13.md) exact original retention, [M14](M14.md) pending receipt review, and [SI2](../improvements/SI2.md) audited revision patterns. ## Frozen Product & Architecture Decisions

### D1 · External sales are separate from native Invoices

Add an **External Sales** navigation entry and a separate business record. Do not represent an imported original as a newly issued `Invoice` or use M13 artifact upload as an accounting-import shortcut.

Retain the issuer/source, original number and original bytes. Allocate no native invoice number and create no native Invoice, Payment, Advance/Final or Credit-chain event. A self-billing original can be entered as a sales invoice using its original number; ordinary invoicing still requires consecutive numbering in one or more series, not merely arbitrary unique identifiers. See the [official self-billing rules](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/aangepaste_regels_facturen/factuur_uitgereikt_door_afnemer) and [invoice requirements](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/btw/administratie_bijhouden/facturen_maken/factuureisen/factuureisen).

### D2 · One original, multiple tax summaries

- Header: source, original number, document date, optional covered period, counterparty, description and `include_in_pl`.
- Rows: explicit sales-side tax treatment, rate, original net amount and original VAT amount. Product/quantity/unit-price/discount detail remains in the original.
- Confirmed original amounts are raw authority inputs. Backend services normalize Decimal amounts to cents and derive totals; the native pricing engine must not silently replace the original VAT with a recalculation.
- A mismatch between rate-derived VAT and the entered original produces a review warning; posting requires acknowledgment with a reason. Rates/categories remain data-driven records with frozen snapshots, not hardcoded tax enums.
- Users confirm tax treatment and P/L inclusion. AI cannot decide either field authoritatively. Only NL domestic ordinary sales treatments are eligible; reject EU/export/reverse-charge/ICP-bearing treatments in this command.

### D3 · Sources, identity and duplicate protection

Sources are company-owned editable records classified as `PLATFORM`, `ENERGY` or `OTHER`, with an optional default P/L inclusion. Source deactivation prevents new selections without invalidating history.

Retain the exact displayed original number and enforce source-scoped uniqueness on its trimmed value; a pending record may omit it, posting may not. Source/number identity remains reserved after posting or voiding. Internal UUIDs, original numbers and native invoice numbers are different identifiers.

Posting uses a lock and expected version; retry/concurrent confirmation never creates another accounting fact. Matching file hashes produce a duplicate warning, not an absolute file-identity constraint: an energy statement can legitimately evidence separate purchase and sale components.

### D4 · Pending review and atomic posting

External sales use `PENDING / POSTED / VOID`. Manual entry can save a pending record without a file; posting requires at least one original. Reuse M14's PNG/JPEG/WebP/PDF validation, per-file configured limit and maximum of five files. Original filenames are display metadata only.

Pending records have no report effect. Confirmation revalidates all required fields and atomically stores the amounts/tax snapshots, originals' ownership and posting history. A repeated or losing concurrent confirmation returns a stable `409` and the existing record identity.

Revenue and VAT use the document date, following existing invoice-date reporting. Covered periods are evidence, not another recognition date. Payout dates do not create revenue again.

### D5 · Platforms and energy companies share the entry flow

Platform business sales default to P/L inclusion; energy sales default to exclusion. Defaults are copied into the entry and reviewed at posting. Later source-setting changes never rewrite posted records.

Only the sale-of-electricity component and its VAT enter external sales. Household energy purchases, fixed charges and the bill's net payable amount are not automatically sales. Post monthly actual settlement evidence, not unsubstantiated payment advances. An annual statement contributes only the manually verified difference from previously posted monthly amounts, with the covered period and adjustment explanation; no automatic annual reconciliation is introduced.

Record platform fees separately as Expenses when supported by their own evidence. Do not infer sales from the net payout or assume the customer invoice equals the worker's remuneration. Zoofy's [professional FAQ](https://zoofy.nl/vakman/veelgestelde-vragen-vakman/) describes a remuneration PDF for bookkeeping and calls the process “reversed billing”; a generic external-document workflow must accommodate the actual evidence without assuming a particular commission structure.

### D6 · Audited revisions, credits and voids

- Posted amounts, dates, tax selections and P/L inclusion may change only through a revision command requiring a reason and expected version. Store immutable before/after business snapshots, actor and time in the same transaction.
- Revisions are history, not additional report events. Reports project one current effective record. Show affected old/new periods; do not claim to know whether they were filed.
- Retain confirmed originals. Additional supporting evidence can be appended; posted originals cannot be deleted or replaced. Financial corrections supported by a new credit/settlement document belong in a separate entry, not an attachment pretending to revise the original.
- Real sales reductions use an external credit document. Energy-settlement adjustments accept evidence-supported signed amounts. Recognize their signs exactly once, not through both negative rows and a second credit sign inversion.
- Link a known original external sale within the same company. If it predates the system, retain its original number and an explanation. External credits do not enter native M12 credit/refund chains.
- Voiding is for a mistaken/duplicate bookkeeping entry, with a required reason and retained evidence/history. It removes the current report contribution; an actual refund is not represented by voiding.
- Pending entries can be discarded through their normal cleanup path. Posted or voided entries have no hard-delete operation.

### D7 · Expense cost and VAT are independent

Add `include_in_pl` and typed `pl_cost_basis = NET | GROSS | AUTO` to Expense and recurring templates, including their write/read contracts and generated Expenses.

`NET` uses the persisted net amount; `GROSS` uses the persisted gross amount. `AUTO` uses net plus actual charged purchase VAT that is irrecoverable under the selected supported treatment and deduction eligibility. Do not add reverse-charge self-assessment or private-use output VAT to the basis again. Apply the existing business percentage, depreciation allocation and per-expense rounding afterwards. This is cost recognition for otherwise eligible business costs, not automatic legal approval of every expenditure.

Legacy Expenses/templates and omitted fields from old API clients retain `include_in_pl=true` and `NET`. New-entry UI defaults to `AUTO`. Editing unrelated legacy fields preserves the stored mode; switching a historical record is explicit and previews its effect. This preserves the older gross-as-net/VAT-zero workaround without interpreting its historical intent.

Propagate both fields through normal Expense entry, recurring templates/generation and M14 confirmation. Mileage projections keep their existing fixed cost behavior. `paid_by` remains a payment-source indicator and does not determine VAT/cost eligibility.

### D8 · Dedicated battery registration

Provide a battery-registration entry from Expense, associated with exactly one acquisition Expense. Store acquisition basis, original VAT/rate snapshots, in-service date and a versioned calculation rule. Registration is not another purchase: input VAT still enters 5b only through the Expense, and the acquisition is excluded from company P/L.

This milestone covers eligible home batteries under the ordinary VAT regime with full initial purchase-VAT deduction. The owner explicitly confirms applicability. KOR, partially deductible acquisitions, disposal and special use changes require a separately designed extension; do not silently run this rule for them.

Registered acquisitions are excluded from the old generic private-use calculation to prevent double adjustment. Keep P/L business-use inputs separate from annual battery private-use ratios. Registering a historical acquisition previews affected report periods and requires confirmation; no automatic historical asset discovery.

Block generic Expense deletion or changes to registered financial/date/tax fields. Those changes go through the audited asset command and update the linked acquisition consistently.

### D9 · Annual private use and five-year calculation

For the in-service calendar year and the four following years, collect a separate annual private-use percentage, required calculation-basis note and optional EMS/energy-bill evidence. Explicit zero is valid; a missing value is not zero. No EMS API or universal automatically inferred percentage is provided.

Calculate in backend services from the acquisition snapshot:

```text
annual private-use base = purchase net basis / 5 × annual private-use percentage
annual private-use VAT  = unrounded annual private-use base × purchase VAT rate
```

Calculate both amounts using unrounded intermediate values, then round each per asset to cents before aggregation. Recognize once in that year's Q4. Do not additionally prorate the first year by purchase month or bind the five-year period to the P/L depreciation field. From year six this acquisition no longer generates this adjustment; electricity-sale VAT continues.

The [official home-battery example](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/btw/content/thuisbatterij-btw) gives €10,000 net, 21% VAT and 45% private use: box 1d base €900 and VAT €189. The percentage must be supported by EMS information, optionally combined with energy statements, and may differ each year.

Annual inputs require confirmation. A Q4 report with missing/unconfirmed applicable annual data returns explicit missing items, `is_complete=false` and clearly labeled known subtotals. Reading a report never creates or confirms annual records.

Asset-basis and annual-input revisions retain audit history. A basis-changing asset revision makes affected years require reconfirmation, preserving earlier evidence and computations; it neither reports stale calculations as current nor submits tax-return corrections.

### D10 · Consistent reports and incorporated SI3

- Include posted external sales' signed domestic VAT contributions once regardless of P/L inclusion. Include revenue in P/L only when `include_in_pl=true`. Pending/void records and audit versions have no contribution.
- Add typed external-source references and navigable audit rows; do not mislabel an external record as a native Invoice. Preserve existing native tax events and ICP behavior.
- Extend box 1d with its taxable base. If a nonzero legacy contribution has no reliably determined base, return the aggregate base as unknown with a warning, retaining the VAT total and the known battery breakdown. Never display an unknown base as zero.
- Add `mileage_expense_actual`, `other_expense_actual`, `profit_before_mileage` and `profit_after_mileage` to P/L totals and each monthly/quarterly series item. Preserve `profit` as profit after mileage. All values use the same selected period, base currency and rounded cost slices.
- Invariants: `expense_actual = mileage_expense_actual + other_expense_actual`; `profit_before_mileage - mileage_expense_actual = profit_after_mileage = profit`; series sum to report totals. Identify mileage by `Expense.kind`, not a mutable category name.
- Expense by Category remains a raw-entry report, with an added P/L-inclusion filter. Default preserves the existing all-entry scope; Dashboard cost rankings explicitly select P/L-included entries. Raw purchase totals are not a promise to equal depreciated P/L costs.
- Do not label either profit as automatically suitable for a particular income-tax field. SI3's recalled “Business Mileage” filing screen is not a dependency: deliver the accounting breakdown without inventing filing guidance.

### D11 · Tax and legacy boundaries

The author chooses to exclude household electricity activity from this company's P/L. Do not turn that bookkeeping selection into a finding that electricity gains have no income-tax consequences. VAT and income-tax activity classification differ; [the Tax Administration assesses income-tax activities and their relationship separately](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/wanneer_bent_u_ondernemer_voor_de_inkomstenbelasting/).

M16 uses the current company's existing single VAT-return context. It does not create another company/registration or resume M15. These decisions incrementally extend M8.5 cost handling, M10 reporting and M14 confirmation; do not rewrite their historical acceptance conclusions or generalize the battery-specific rule to all assets.

## Contract (Define First)

All routes are under `/api/v1`, require the existing MFA owner identity, derive company ownership on the server and use PostgreSQL RLS. Money is Decimal serialized as strings. Expected-version/state/duplicate conflicts return stable `409` codes; invalid inputs `422`; missing/cross-company objects `404` before file/AI access. List/history endpoints use existing pagination conventions.

| Interface | Operations |
| --- | --- |
| `/external-sales-sources` | GET/POST collection; PUT item, including deactivation |
| `/external-sales` | POST pending entry; GET collection/detail; PUT pending fields; DELETE pending entry only |
| `/external-sales/{id}/files` | POST originals, GET metadata/content; DELETE a pending original only; append supporting evidence after posting |
| `/external-sales/{id}/extract` | POST advisory extraction/retry; expose safe state and parsed suggestions in detail |
| `/external-sales/{id}/calculate`, `/confirm` | POST backend preview and expected-version atomic posting |
| `/external-sales/{id}/revisions`, `/void` | POST reasoned version-checked revision/void; GET revision history |
| `/battery-assets` | POST acquisition association; GET asset collection/detail |
| `/battery-assets/{id}/annual-use/{year}` | GET/PUT annual input, POST `/calculate` and `/confirm`, POST/GET `/revisions` |
| `/battery-assets/{id}/revisions` | POST audited asset-basis revision; GET history |

External sales distinguish ordinary sale, external credit and settlement adjustment. Signed rows and the document kind have one shared sign convention. Drafts allow incomplete fields; posting requires the complete closed write schema, source identity, original number, originals and confirmed tax/P&L choices. Client-calculated totals are never accepted as authority.

An advisory AI schema carries optional observed header/summary amounts, confidence and safe review notes. It carries no authoritative tax-treatment or P/L decision. Persist only validated suggestions and safe errors. Reuse M14 file-revision/run-generation checks; stale extraction cannot overwrite edited fields, a changed file group or a posted record.

Existing Expense/recurring/M14 contracts gain the D7 fields. Reporting contracts gain P/L breakdowns, source references, box 1d base and missing-input/completeness information. Regenerate committed `schema.d.ts` whenever a step changes the contract. Full annual validation, cost selection, tax snapshots and report calculations live in `services/`.

## Data Models / Migrations

- Add company-owned external source, external sale header, normalized tax-summary rows, original/supporting file metadata and append-only audit tables. Store source/number identity, typed kind/state, dates, P/L inclusion, frozen amounts/tax selections, version, actor and timestamps.
- Add battery asset, annual-use and append-only audit tables. Each asset uniquely references its acquisition; each asset/year has one current input/version with confirmed computation snapshots and the asset version it used. Protect linked acquisitions with FK/command guards. Keep annual evidence in dedicated ownership relations.
- Reuse storage validation/transport components, not native `document_artifact` identity. Preserve original bytes; controlled UUID keys, authenticated reads, upload compensation and safe cleanup follow M14. Do not build a wide attachment/tax table with many nullable parent FKs.
- New core rows carry `company_id`, RLS/runtime-role permissions, ownership constraints and suitable source/number and asset/year uniqueness. Audits are append-only. Use DB/ORM cascades for children; use RESTRICT where retained posted evidence must prevent deletion. No hand-written relational cascade cleanup.
- Add Expense/recurring fields with legacy-preserving defaults. Append migrations to the actual `main` Alembic head at implementation time; never select paused M15's head. Do not alter historical numbers, amounts, files, audit versions or infer old battery registrations.
- Cover fresh creation and production-shaped upgrades. Downgrades must refuse to discard retained posted/audit data rather than silently destroy it.
