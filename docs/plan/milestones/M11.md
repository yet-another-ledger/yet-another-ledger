# M11 · Mileage Expenses (Private Transport Used for Business)

> 🌐 **English** · [中文](M11_zh.md)

> **Status: 🟢 Done · frozen with author on 2026-08-19; implementation completed 2026-08-20; author walkthrough accepted 2026-08-21.** This milestone adds a business-mileage workflow for privately owned or privately rented transport. It does **not** implement company-vehicle accounting. Read `docs/plan/roadmap.md` §2 global constraints plus the M8/M8.5/M10 expense and reporting decisions before implementation.
>
> **Tax anchor (current at planning time):** the Dutch Tax Authority states that private transport used for business may be deducted at **€0.25/km in 2026**; the amount was **€0.23/km in 2024 and 2025**. For income tax, commuting (`woon-werkverkeer`) counts as business mileage. The allowance includes costs such as fuel, insurance, tolls, and parking, so those costs must not be deducted again through this mileage claim. Source: [Belastingdienst · Zakelijk gebruik privévervoermiddel](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/inkomstenbelasting_voor_ondernemers/zakelijk-gebruik-privevervoermiddel).

> **Current source extension (2026-11-10)** [M18 Agent mileage intake](M18.md) is implemented in the current source tree under a separate `mileage:create` permission. Integrated gates and author walkthrough remain pending; see [M18](M18.md) and the [roadmap](../roadmap.md) for current delivery status. This does not change M11's accepted scope or mileage calculations.

## Dependencies and Existing Capabilities

- **M8 / M8.5 Expense foundation**: `expense` already feeds P/L, Dashboard, Expense Report, and BTW reporting; it carries category/tax/currency snapshots plus `paid_by`, `business_percentage`, and `depreciation_years`.
- **M4 data-driven dictionaries**: expense categories, VAT rates, and VAT treatments are company-scoped editable data. M11 adds an equally data-driven transport-type dictionary.
- **M7.5 money convention**: user-facing amounts are rounded to the currency minor unit with `Decimal` and `ROUND_HALF_UP`; intermediate distance/rate multiplication retains at least three decimal places.
- **M10 reporting**: a mileage claim must appear as a business cost in P/L and normal expense totals, while contributing zero VAT to the BTW return.
- **Typed settings**: company defaults use the existing three-tier settings service and Pydantic models; no raw string settings.

## Goals and Scope

- **Goal**: Record business mileage travelled using private transport by entering the trip date and one-way distance, optionally marking it as a return trip; calculate and persist the deductible expense from effective-dated, user-editable rates.
- **In Scope (IN)**:
  - All business trips made with privately owned or privately rented transport, including commuting, customer visits, supplier visits, and project-site travel.
  - Company-maintained transport types, seeded with Car / Motorcycle / Bicycle / Other; Car is the default.
  - A general effective-dated mileage-rate schedule plus optional transport-type-specific schedules.
  - A dedicated Mileage expense category; no conversion of existing expenses or the existing Travel category.
  - A mileage trip record linked to an automatically generated Expense, so all existing financial reports continue to use the Expense projection.
  - Optional origin address, destination address, trip purpose, and note; manual one-way kilometres remain authoritative.
  - Explicit preview + confirmed bulk recalculation when a rate is changed retrospectively, with an audit trail.
  - Expense page tabs: Purchase expenses and Mileage expenses, each with its own columns and editor.
- **Out of Scope / Deferred (OUT)**:
  - Company-owned/company-leased vehicle accounting. Those vehicles use actual-cost accounting; their trip logs serve different BTW/bijtelling purposes and must not generate this flat-rate Expense. The trip model leaves an additive extension point for this later.
  - Google Places Autocomplete / Routes API or any other address/routing provider. A future follow-on may add per-instance credentials, backend proxying, attribution, suggested route distance, and manual fallback; M11 stores optional address text only.
  - Odometer-based closed journey registration, private detours, multiple intermediate stops, GPS tracking, vehicle asset register, recurring commute templates, and mileage export files.
  - Automatic retrieval of statutory rates. Seed values are editable data and carry no promise of future legal updates.

## Frozen Product and Architecture Decisions

- [x] **D1 · Product term and boundary**: UI term = **Mileage expense / 里程支出（私人交通工具商用）**. It covers all business mileage with private transport, not only commuting and not only cars.
- [x] **D2 · Private first, company vehicle later**: M11 public write APIs create only `PRIVATE` trips. The trip row has its own identity and a nullable one-to-one Expense link so a later company-vehicle workflow can reuse trip records without creating a flat-rate Expense.
- [x] **D3 · Expense remains the reporting projection**: each M11 private trip atomically creates one `expense(kind=MILEAGE)`. Existing report services continue reading Expense; they do not union a second financial table.
- [x] **D4 · Existing data untouched**: the migration backfills every existing Expense as `PURCHASE`; no amount, category, VAT snapshot, attachment, recurring template, or report history is recomputed. Existing Travel remains user-managed.
- [x] **D5 · Separate page tabs**: keep one top-level Expenses navigation item; its page has `Purchase expenses` and `Mileage expenses` tabs. Purchase retains the existing receipt-oriented table/editor; Mileage gets distance/rate-oriented columns and a compact editor.
- [x] **D6 · Entry semantics**: trip date, transport type, and one-way distance (>0) are required. `round_trip=false` by default; when true, backend `total_distance_km = one_way_distance_km × 2`. Origin/destination/purpose/note are optional `text`.
- [x] **D7 · Data-driven transport types**: company-scoped transport types are editable records, not a hardcoded enum. Seed Car / Motorcycle / Bicycle / Other; Car is the configured default. Inactive types remain readable historically but cannot be selected for new trips.
- [x] **D8 · Effective-dated rate hierarchy**: a rate rule has `effective_from` and optional `transport_type_id`. Resolve the newest type-specific rule whose date is `<= trip_date`; otherwise resolve the newest general rule. No valid rule is a configuration error, never an implicit zero.
- [x] **D9 · Initial editable seeds**: general rules seed `2024-01-01 → 0.230` and `2026-01-01 → 0.250` in base-currency units per kilometre. Transport types inherit them unless a type-specific rule overrides them.
- [x] **D10 · Snapshot history**: each trip stores the selected type name, rule id (nullable historical FK), effective date, rate per kilometre, total distance, and calculated amount. Ordinary rule edits never silently mutate saved trips.
- [x] **D11 · Retrospective correction is explicit**: after adding/editing/deleting a past-effective rule, the user previews mismatching trips and total delta, then explicitly applies. Apply revalidates an opaque preview token; stale previews return `409` and require a fresh preview.
- [x] **D12 · Rate-adjustment audit**: each applied correction stores trip id, old/new rule snapshots, old/new rate, old/new amount, actor, and timestamp. Applying updates trip + linked Expense + audit rows in one transaction.
- [x] **D13 · Mileage category**: seed a new per-company `Mileage` expense category (`default_deductible=false`, matching the no-input-VAT treatment) for existing and future companies, set it as the company mileage default, and allow the owner to choose another live category in settings. Existing expenses are never moved into it.
- [x] **D14 · Accounting projection**: generated Expense uses company base currency, `exchange_rate=1`, `net=gross=mileage amount`, `vat=0`, `deductible=false`, `paid_by=PRIVATE`, `business_percentage=100`, `depreciation_years=1`, and no supplier/receipt requirement. Here `deductible=false` is the existing **input-VAT** flag—there is no input VAT to reclaim; it does not prevent the amount from being recognised as an income-tax business cost in P/L.
- [x] **D15 · VAT representation is data-driven**: seed a PURCHASE-side treatment `NL_PRIVATE_TRANSPORT_MILEAGE` (`effect=EXEMPT`, `deductible=false`, no report box) and use the company zero VAT rate; snapshots make the €0 VAT state explicit. If required VAT master data is missing, creation fails with a clear configuration error rather than inventing a code in service logic.
- [x] **D16 · Money math only in services**: frontend sends date/type/distance/round-trip and optional text. Backend computes total distance, resolves the rate, calculates the amount, and quantises once at the final amount to the currency minor unit.
- [x] **D17 · Edit/delete invariants**: only mileage endpoints may change a Mileage Expense's financial fields; generic Expense PUT rejects `MILEAGE`. Deleting the Expense root uses DB cascade to remove the linked private trip/audit rows; no hand-written child cascade.
- [x] **D18 · External maps deferred cleanly**: no provider keys, coordinates, place ids, provider response caches, or external calls land in M11. Future route suggestions may prefill distance, but the user-confirmed distance remains the saved authority.

## Contract (Upfront)

> Exact names may follow existing singular/plural conventions, but the shapes and behaviours below are frozen. Every contract change regenerates `frontend/src/api/schema.d.ts`.

### Existing Expense contract extensions

- `ExpenseKind = "PURCHASE" | "MILEAGE"` added to `ExpenseRead` and `ExpenseListItem`.
- `GET /api/v1/expenses` adds optional `kind`; the Purchase tab requests `PURCHASE` so Mileage rows never leak into the receipt-oriented table.
- Existing `POST /expenses` creates only `PURCHASE`; existing `PUT /expenses/{id}` returns `409` for a Mileage row and points callers to the mileage endpoint. Existing DELETE remains the Expense-root deletion path.

### Mileage defaults, transport types, and rates

- `GET/PUT /api/v1/settings/mileage-defaults` → `MileageDefaultsRead/Update { expense_category_id, default_transport_type_id }` (COMPANY level, owner-only; referenced rows must be live and belong to the company).
- CRUD `/api/v1/mileage-transport-types` with `MileageTransportTypeWrite { name, active }` and read/list models; unique non-empty name per company. The configured default cannot be deleted/deactivated until another default is selected.
- CRUD `/api/v1/mileage-rates` with `MileageRateWrite { transport_type_id?, effective_from, rate_per_km }`; `transport_type_id=null` means general rate. `(company, transport_type-or-general, effective_from)` is unique; rate must be `>0`.

### Calculation and Mileage CRUD

- `POST /api/v1/mileage-expenses/calculate` with `MileageCalculationRequest { trip_date, transport_type_id?, one_way_distance_km, round_trip }` → `MileageCalculationRead { one_way_distance_km, total_distance_km, rate_rule_id, rate_effective_from, rate_per_km, amount, currency }`; no persistence.
- `POST /api/v1/mileage-expenses` with the calculation inputs plus optional `origin_address`, `destination_address`, `purpose`, `note` → `201 MileageExpenseRead` (trip + linked Expense id/category/amount snapshots).
- `GET /api/v1/mileage-expenses` supports `q`, `transport_type_id`, inclusive date range, pagination, and date/created sorting; returns purpose/address/type/distance/rate/amount columns required by the Mileage tab.
- `GET/PUT/DELETE /api/v1/mileage-expenses/{trip_id}` are company-scoped. PUT recomputes from raw inputs and writes a rate-adjustment audit row only when the resolved rate changes.
- `GET /api/v1/mileage-expenses/{trip_id}/rate-adjustments` returns the chronological audit list.

### Retrospective recalculation

- `POST /api/v1/mileage-expenses/rate-recalculation/preview` compares all company Mileage trips with the current effective schedules and returns `preview_token`, affected count, old/new totals, delta, and paginated row details. Type-specific overrides shield matching trips from unrelated general-rate changes.
- `POST /api/v1/mileage-expenses/rate-recalculation/apply` with `{ preview_token }` locks/rechecks the affected rows, returns `409` if the preview is stale, otherwise atomically updates trips + Expense projections + audit rows and returns the actual summary.

## Data Model and Migration

- **`expense.kind`**: typed `PURCHASE|MILEAGE`, `NOT NULL`, server default `PURCHASE`, indexed with company/date as useful for tab filtering.
- **`mileage_transport_type`**: `id`, `company_id`, `name` (`text`), `active`, timestamps; unique `(company_id, name)`.
- **`mileage_rate`**: `id`, `company_id`, nullable `transport_type_id`, `effective_from`, `rate_per_km NUMERIC`, timestamps; separate uniqueness for general and type-specific effective dates. Type delete cascades its override rules; historical trips retain snapshots.
- **`mileage_trip`**: own `id` + `company_id`; nullable unique `expense_id` FK with `ON DELETE CASCADE`; ownership fixed to PRIVATE by M11 API; type/rate FKs `SET NULL` with snapshots; trip date, one-way/total distance, round-trip flag, calculated amount, optional text fields, creator/timestamps.
- **`mileage_rate_adjustment`**: company/trip, old/new rule/rate/amount snapshots, actor/timestamp; trip FK `ON DELETE CASCADE`.
- **Typed company setting**: `expense.mileage.defaults` holds category/default-type UUIDs; service validates ownership/activity on read and write.
- **Additive migration (next linear revision after current head)**:
  - Adds tables/column/indexes/FKs only; existing Expense rows receive `PURCHASE` without recomputation.
  - Inserts or reuses (by company/name or stable code) the Mileage category, transport types, default general rates, no-input-VAT treatment, and company defaults for every existing company; onboarding seeds the same set idempotently for future companies.
  - Does not delete or rename Travel and does not infer any existing Expense as Mileage.

## Calculation and Persistence Rules

1. Validate live company category/type and a positive finite Decimal distance.
2. Derive `total_distance_km` from the round-trip flag; retain three-decimal distance precision.
3. Resolve rate by D8 and snapshot the selected rule/type.
4. Compute `raw_amount = total_distance_km × rate_per_km`; quantise only the final monetary amount through `quantize_to_minor_unit`.
5. Create/update the Expense projection with the fixed accounting fields in D14 and linked category/VAT snapshots.
6. Commit trip and Expense atomically. Any lookup/calculation failure leaves neither row persisted.
